# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in WeGotWorkspace.

## What is open today

| Kind | Status |
|------|--------|
| **Bug reports** | Open — use the [Bug report](https://github.com/WeGotWorkspace/wegotworkspace/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) template |
| **Security / DAST findings** | Open — use the DAST finding template where applicable |
| **Ideas / feature requests / Goals** | **Closed** to the public until the legal entity / CLA exists |
| **Code contributions (PRs from outside maintainers)** | **Closed** until CLA |

Do not open issues to propose product Goals or large features yet. Discussions exist for eventual intake; they are **not** announced as a community hub until maintainers deliberately open that phase. Maintainers may still explore in Discussions and promote Ideas to Goals at Product Status **Accepted**.

## Product vs engineering

- **Goals** (`type:goal`) describe user outcomes — maintainers only for now. Board Status is **Accepted → Building → Shipped**. See [docs/product/](docs/product/) and [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
- **Epics / tasks / chores** (`type:epic` / `type:task` / `type:chore`) track delivery under Goals or pure eng work.
- Templates: `goal.yml`, `epic.yml`, `task.yml`, `chore.yml`, `bug-report.yml`, `dast-finding.yml` under [`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/).
- The legacy `roadmap` label has been removed — use `type:goal` + the Product roadmap Project.

## Maintainers

Internal work follows [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), and agent skills under `.agents/skills/`.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful. Harassment and bad-faith reports are not welcome. A fuller CoC may land with the legal entity.
